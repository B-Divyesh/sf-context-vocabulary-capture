# Demo sandbox

Open `/demo` or the one-click catalog URL `/?demo=1` to seed three realistic vocabulary captures. The persistent banner says “Demo — sample data, nothing is saved.” It offers **Reset demo** and **Start for real**.

Demo records use the `demo:keep-the-sentence:vault` and `demo:keep-the-sentence:device-key` local-storage namespaces. Real records use `keep-the-sentence:vault` and `keep-the-sentence:device-key`. **Start for real** clears the demo records before leaving and never creates a real-data key. The demo makes no product data requests.

The sample includes English, Spanish, and German phrases with source context, a source link, and learner meanings. Use **Mark phrase as remembered** to record a review or **Export CSV** to download the records.
